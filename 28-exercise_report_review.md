## Exercise: Review the review
Participants should read the
["2023 Microsoft Online Exchange Incident"](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2025-03/CSRBReviewOfTheSummer2023MEOIntrusion508.pdf)
from CSRB and answer the following questions:
- What happened (high-level)?
- What were the consequences (for Microsoft and/or their customers)?
- What could Microsoft have done to prevent/detect the intrusion (high-level)?
- What are your most important takeaways?
