## Exercise: Lessons learned
Participants are split into groups.

Each group will be assigned an
article, press release or similar.

The groups should discuss and answer
the following questions:
- What happened (summary)?
- Important takeaways/lessons learned?
- Alternative motives (source criticism)?

After presentation, send notes to:
**[courses+ti_012901@%EMAIL_DOMAIN%](mailto:courses+ti_012901@%EMAIL_DOMAIN%)**
